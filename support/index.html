Frequently asked questions
How do I play online with friends?

Choose an online game (for example Trivia, Liar or Intuition Edge), create a table and share the room code or invite friends from your friends list. Your friends tap “Room code” on the home screen and enter the code.

How do I add friends?

Open Friends from the home screen and add a friend by their profile ID. They will see your request in Notifications.

How do I manage or cancel Premium?

Premium subscriptions are handled by the store you bought them from:

  • iPhone / iPad: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → GERAPPA.
  • Android: Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → GERAPPA.

Refunds are processed by Apple or Google according to their policies. If something went wrong with a purchase, write to us and we will help.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: open your profile (the settings button on the home screen) → “Delete account” and confirm. Your account and personal data are deleted. You can also email us from the address linked to your account and we will delete it for you.

The app is not working correctly. What should I do?

Make sure you have the latest version from the App Store or Google Play and a stable internet connection, then restart the app. If the problem continues, email us a short description and, if possible, a screenshot.
